# All-Around Competitor

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/items/all-around-competitor
- Release: Season of the Chosen

Complete 3 playlist activities (strikes, Crucible, or Gambit) with your Guardian Games class item equipped.

Defeat 300 targets in any playlist with your Guardian Games class item equipped. Defeating opponents in Crucible or Gambit grants the most efficient progress.
