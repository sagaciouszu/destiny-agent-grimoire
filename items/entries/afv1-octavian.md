# AFv1 Octavian

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/items/afv1-octavian
- Release: Destiny/The Dark Below (2015.05.09)

The AFv1 Octavian is an advanced Phaeton-class, long range jumpship. Upgraded for improved performance and style, the Octavian is as close to the Phaeton's original factory specs as resources will allow.
