# Act II: Rites of the Deep

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/items/act-ii-rites-of-the-deep-4
- Release: Heresy

You'll need to find at least three shards of Willbreaker to reforge it. Acquire at least 3 "Willbreaker" quests from the Shaping Slab vendor.

Choosing to find additional shards will make it slightly easier to reforge Willbreaker.
