# Denier of Futurity

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/entries/denier-of-futurity

It happens as Bael withdraws from the Luna operation. He is alone in his ship when he feels chill fog move through the valley of his heart, and for a moment he startles at the revealed warmth of his own blood. His new, titanium hand instinctively grabs at his chest, searching for something that is no longer there.

"VI?"

Bael feels himself sweating.

"Answer me!"

VI's voice rumbles on the edge of his perception, the sound of it carrying with the cold. THE FUTURE BEYOND THIS NODE OF TIME IS OPAQUE, says negative 177 Celsius, THE ENDEAVOR OF OUR ALTERNATE WEAPON HAS FAILED

"What do you mean?"

Bael crumples over his controls with the weight of the vision of a future that will no longer occur: himself, blade high, the Lightbearers dead and the false Weapon dead. This other-him is triumphant, his City is free, and he holds out his hand as if holding a sidearm, and with VI in his fingertips time bends and he shoots a meteor straight through the Traveler's center. He smiles as the corpse crashes through the atmosphere, feels the soundwave as it makes impact, tithes the kill to VI, and VI uses that tithe to tear a hole in the sky. VI escapes and he is free and Bael has killed god and he is also free.

But the voice says, THIS FUTURE IS NO LONGER POSSIBLE

And in this now, VI leaves Bael with only that vision as a token and, one final time, departs his body without a further word. The chill is gone, and for the first time in years, Bael's skin warms.
