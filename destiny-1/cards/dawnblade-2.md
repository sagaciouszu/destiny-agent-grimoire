# Dawnblade

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/entries/dawnblade-2

Rise. Closer, closer to the sun.
Your life shall be restored; the three shall be as one.

Dash. Swiftly, swiftly as the Light.
A scorched earth lies below; foes flee from the fight.

An incandescent ray; behold the mighty way.

Rise. Closer, closer to the blaze.
My wings shall never melt. They're already aflame.
