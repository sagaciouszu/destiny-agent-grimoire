# Liturgy

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/entries/liturgy

it pins the   screeb

quivering pustules

it chants

iirsoveks - di

it cuts skin   pain shriek

spurt of   dark ether

squirming

shas'ki   iirsoveks

it fastens chain to chain

to chain to chain

di   di   di

it claws pustule

tear   skin

shriek shriek   shriek

iirsoveks – di

di   di   di

skull   crack

silent

it pries open

shas'ki   iirsoveks

to chain to chain to   blade

it makes a circle

di   di   di

alive now

a flicker of   flame
