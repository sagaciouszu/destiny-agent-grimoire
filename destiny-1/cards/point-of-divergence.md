# Point of Divergence

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/entries/point-of-divergence

Ikora lifts a data-pad from the desk in Zavala's office, as she adjusts her posture within the permanent impression left in his chair. Once eye level, the pad scans the Warlock Vanguard's retinal pattern and unlocks. A single, blinking message rises above the rest.

Vanguard Rey,

We've found her. I brought this to Osiris and Rahool, for interpretation, when our network probes intercepted a burst of transmissions between the Vex Collective and the Nessian Schism. We didn't get much; we had to cut our connection to the probes once the net started to assimilate them. I wish I had more to show for all the effort it took to breach the Collective's communications in the first place, but at least it's something. And—as best we can tell—the source is ricocheting between Earth and some planetoid on the outer extent of Sol—but not Nessus.

-Aunor
