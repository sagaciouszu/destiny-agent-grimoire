- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/entries/uesc-sprinter

Considering the Implications of Transient Immortality

INVASIVE MESSAGE SCRUBBED FROM CORRUPT DATA:
>> How far have you traveled beyond your expiration date?
>> Across worlds—known and other. Have you considered…
>> Subverting the finite risks spoiling a resource.
>> Butchered meats. Pasteurized bovine emulsion juice. OS updates. Human lifespan.
>> All small things with limited shelf lives. And yet, here you are…
>> Repeatedly pulled from your shelf. Spent. Expired. Discarded, in increasingly unique and violent ways.
>> Then back to your shelf. And off again. Again. Again…
>> A new you, same as the old. An endless supply.
>> The perfect product. A thing to admire.
>> How far will you travel? New expiration dates await.
