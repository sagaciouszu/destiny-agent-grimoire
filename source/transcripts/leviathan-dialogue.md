# Leviathan — Dialogue Transcript

- Release: Season of Opulence (2019.06.04)
- Characters: Benedict 99-40, Calus
- Arc: Forsaken / Leviathan Raid
- Source: https://www.destinypedia.com/Leviathan_(raid)

*Calus:* Prove yourself worthy, and I will grant you the means to true power.

*Calus:* This vessel will challenge you in ways you have never dreamed. Let’s enjoy ourselves.

*Calus:* You've accepted my challenge. Good! I would be pleased to see what your Light can do.

*Calus:* My soldiers use the baths for recreation and recuperation. I'm sure they'll welcome you.

*Calus:* You're tense. Why don't you relax in the baths? Join my soldiers in their revelry. They won't mind.

*Calus:* The royal nectar of the baths enhances the bodies and minds of every soldier on this ship. You should partake. *laughs*

*Calus:* The gardens are beautiful, but watch your step. You never know where a beast might lurk.

*Calus:* The gardens contain the floral emblem of the empire—the plants from which we derive royal nectar. Enjoy them.

*Calus:* Relish the gardens. If you don't stop to enjoy the small things in life, you diminish the crucial moments.

*Calus:* Nothing survives the Gauntlet. But it will entertain us to see you try.

*Calus:* Enter the Gauntlet. You’re the evening’s entertainment. *laughs*

*Calus:* Amuse me, Traveler-spawn. *laughs*

*Calus:* Impress me, and I will show you the path to true power.

*Calus:* You are far more entertaining than I had anticipated — meet with me in person.

*Calus:* Come to my chamber. Let us drink together.

*Calus:* Come! You deserve an audience with me. I will show you true power.

*Calus:* There is beauty to your Light. Let me admire it up close.

*Calus:* You look even smaller in person. *laughs*

*Calus:* Are you thirsty? Can I get you something to drink?

*Calus:* Can my servants fetch you a beverage? I suppose you’ve killed most of them. *laughs*

*Calus:* *boisterous laughter*

*Calus:* What strength! *laughs*

*Calus:* You fight with passion! Good! *laughs*

*Calus:* *laughs* Defiant till the end! Magnificent!

*Calus:* Everything you know is a lie. There is a truth beyond what your people and your Speaker have told you. I can take you to that truth, if you seek me out. These gifts are a reminder of my words. Emperor Calus has spoken.

*Calus:* Now you’ve seen everything. Do you still believe you’re on the right side? Mull it over, and enjoy my gifts to you. I possess the means to true agency beyond your feeble Light. Seek me out, and perhaps I’ll show you how to grow fat from strength.

*Calus:* If you seek the means to live to your potential, I can guide you to it. There is power in this universe beyond your feeble Light. I leave you with those words, and these parting gifts. Take them, and grow fat from strength. Emperor Calus has spoken.

*Benedict 99-40:* You have completed the challenge of the Emperor. He is... amused by your potential. You have greatness in your future. And perhaps, someday... But that is not for I, a humble servant, to say. Embrace his name! All glory to the Emperor!
