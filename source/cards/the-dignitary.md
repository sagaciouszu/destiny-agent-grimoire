# The Dignitary

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/entries/the-dignitary

A letter addressed to the Vanguard c/o Ikora Rey

RE: Exchange Program

Ikora,

Great news! As per our conversations about the Aionian exchange program—Dean Rebecca enthusiastically accepted the offer! She agreed with your idea that the primary goal would be a cultural exchange, and she's begun fielding applications from the Aionians' School of Anthropology. Would you be opposed to other special-interest applicants as well? The Dean suggested a student from the School of Botany, and I, selfishly, have a Linguistics student I would like to nominate for the exchange.

A logistical consideration: We're not entirely sure if leaving Kepler will have a significant impact on Aionian physiology. My understanding is that exposure to the singularity, the Aionians' "Holy Anomaly," affects their relationship with time. Some of the Aionians are much older than they seem—older than humanly possible, even. The Dean's wary. She and the rest of the Aionians recognize this step as necessary for the progress of humanity, but I can't forget the papers they published on their previous experiments with leaving the planetoid. Dean Rebecca seemed reassured by your plan to have extra medics on hand should the worst happen—all we can do is be prepared.

I'm sure you've got a mental list of folks from the City itching to participate in the exchange. Knowing you, it's already thirty names deep. I can think of at least eight off the top of my head. If anyone else is interested, I can help make the arrangements. We could discuss further over lunch next week?

Yours,

Louis

P.S. Remind me to tell you about the cake I just found.
