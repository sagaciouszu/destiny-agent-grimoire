# Dissected Carve

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/entries/dissected-carve

You guide the Ramulose into alignment with the enemy mothership.

They're probably responding to your Kell's call for aid—just as you are. Your instruments detect multiple life signs blessed with Ether aboard.

You wonder if your Kell has a plan for escape, or how she would ever be taken willingly, and whether you're missing elements of a nuanced plan. Without waiting for orders, you silently key up your systems and power your Arc weapons…

And then, suddenly, the only thing you have in common with yourself is that that's who you used to be—
