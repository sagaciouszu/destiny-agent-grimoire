# A Gift for the Worthy

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/items/a-gift-for-the-worthy
- Release: Season of Opulence

In playlist strikes, get Grenade Launcher multikills, land Grenade Launcher final blows, and earn points by defeating enemy combatants. Death impedes progress, while Grenade Launcher final blows grant the most efficient progress.

This quest's progress is tracked account-wide.
