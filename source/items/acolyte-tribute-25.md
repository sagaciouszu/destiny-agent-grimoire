# Acolyte Tribute (2/5)

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/items/acolyte-tribute-25
- Release: Season of Opulence

A tribute with the power to simulate a strong Hive Acolyte. The second of five available Hive-themed tributes.

Cost may be reduced by delivering Boons of Opulence to the Visage of Calus.
