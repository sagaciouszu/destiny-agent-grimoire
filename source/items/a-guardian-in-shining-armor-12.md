# A Guardian in Shining Armor

- Source: https://www.ishtar-collective.net/items/a-guardian-in-shining-armor-12
- Release: Season of the Deep

Imbue a piece of Sunlit armor with Shining Embers. You can do so from the armor mods of the Sunlit armor.

You may only imbue a Sunlit armor piece with Large Kindling applied as a mod.
